About BLACKPINE FIELD

Our mission

BLACKPINE FIELD exists to put the numbers first. Every product on these shelves is judged on its published specifications — watt-hours, denier, pore size, trigger speed, rated life — and given a straight verdict in plain language. No hype, no invented testing. If we have not used a piece of gear ourselves, we say so. If the numbers do not hold up, we say that too.

The owner

BLACKPINE FIELD is run by Matt Johnson, an outdoorsman from Evarts in Harlan County, Kentucky. He grew up in coal country and hunts the same ridges his family worked, and he picks gear the way he was raised to: by what it does, not what the box promises. The store started because the gear guides he kept finding were either ads in disguise or reviews of things nobody had actually measured. So he built the shelf he wanted to shop from — every product on the numbers, every verdict in plain language.

How we judge

BLACKPINE FIELD publishes research and specification comparisons. We compare published specs — denier, weave, watt-hours, decibels, microns, rated filter life — and label every figure for what it is, including when it's a manufacturer's claim. Unless a piece says otherwise in the text, we have not hands-on tested the products covered, and we never invent test results, runtimes, or field anecdotes. The day a piece of gear is actually in hand and used, the piece will say so — with dates, conditions, and load stated.

Privacy, in plain words

This site has no accounts and no checkout — when you buy something, you buy it on the retailer's own site, under their terms. The only information we collect is the email address you type into the deer-season checklist form, sent to us through FormSubmit so we can email you the PDF. We never sell email addresses, and we never send anything you did not ask for. We do not run our own analytics or tracking. A few pages load a verification script from one of our affiliate networks, and the retailers you click through to may set cookies on their own sites, under their own privacy policies.
