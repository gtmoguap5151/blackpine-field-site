The Season Changes: Tennessee Archery Closes, Kentucky Gun Opens

Two states, two methods, two weeks apart. If you hunt both sides of the line, late October into mid-November is a gear and mindset switch, not just a date change. Here is the transition, the dates that matter, and a short checklist for crossing over.

The dates

Tennessee: the first archery segment closes Friday, October 30, 2026. That is the end of the first segment, not the end of archery — archery reopens November 2–6.

Kentucky: the modern gun season opens Saturday, November 14, 2026, and runs through Sunday, November 29, 2026.

So the gap is two full weeks between the Tennessee bow segment closing and the Kentucky gun opener. Time to re-zero, re-pack, and re-think the sit.

What actually changes

The method. Bow to firearm is a different weapon, a different effective range, and a different way of setting up. What worked at bow range does not automatically carry over.

The pressure. Gun season brings more hunters into the same woods. Expect company, expect movement, and expect the quiet spots from October to feel different in November.

The conditions. Mid-to-late November sits are colder and darker than October ones. The kit that was fine for an October evening can leave you cold by the third hour of a November morning.

The transition checklist

  • Confirm zero with the ammo you will hunt with. Not last year's box, not a different load — the exact ammunition going into the gun on November 14.
  • Re-pack for November. Layers for long, cold sits, and a check that everything you need is still where you think it is after a month in storage.
  • Re-read your state's current guide before you go. Seasons and rules change year to year. What was true last season is not a plan for this one.
  • Know which method is legal where you are standing, on the day you are hunting. The line between states is also a line between seasons. Hunt the season you are actually in.

Two states. One transition. The season changes — your kit should too.

Sources

Sources (checked 2026-10-28): Kentucky modern gun season November 14–29, 2026, per the KDFWR fall-seasons notice; Tennessee early archery first segment September 26 – October 30, 2026, with archery reopening November 2–6, per Tennessee's published 2026–27 season framework.
